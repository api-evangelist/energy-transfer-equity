# Energy Transfer Equity, L.P.

> Energy Transfer Equity, L.P. (NYSE: ETE) was a master limited partnership that
> owned and operated a diverse portfolio of midstream energy assets — natural gas
> gathering, processing, transportation and storage, NGL and crude pipelines, and
> retail fuel. In October 2018 ETE merged with its operating subsidiary Energy
> Transfer Partners, L.P. to form a single publicly traded partnership, Energy
> Transfer LP (NYSE: ET). ETE no longer exists as a separate registrant and
> publishes no API, developer portal, or machine-readable contract of its own.
> Every developer resource that once sat under the ETE umbrella — including the
> Messenger+ portal for pipeline messaging, nominations and gas scheduling — is
> now published under the Energy Transfer LP brand.

This file is an independent third-party profile maintained by API Evangelist. It
is not published by Energy Transfer.

## Status

- Entity status: dissolved into Energy Transfer LP, October 2018
- API surface under this name: none
- Machine-readable contract under this name: none found (see Discovery below)
- Legacy corporate domain energytransferequity.com: no longer company-controlled;
  delegated to GoDaddy NameFind parking nameservers and listed for sale on the
  GoDaddy aftermarket

## Successor

- [Energy Transfer LP](https://www.energytransfer.com): the surviving entity
- [Messenger+ developer portal](https://dev.messenger.energytransfer.com/): the
  successor's login-gated portal for shippers — pipeline messaging, nominations
  and gas scheduling
- [api-evangelist/energy-transfer](https://github.com/api-evangelist/energy-transfer):
  the API Evangelist profile that carries the successor's API artifacts

## Discovery

Probed 2026-09-06 across energytransferequity.com, www.energytransfer.com and
dev.messenger.energytransfer.com:

- OpenAPI / Swagger (/openapi.json, /openapi.yaml, /swagger.json,
  /swagger/v1/swagger.json, /v1/openapi.json, /api-docs, /docs, /redoc): none.
  The successor's /docs, /redoc and /api-docs all return the identical
  2,444,573-byte marketing SPA shell — a soft-404, not documentation.
- A2A agent card (/.well-known/agent-card.json, /.well-known/agent.json): none.
- MCP server: none published.
- /.well-known/ discovery surface: nothing served. Full probe record with per-path
  HTTP statuses in well-known/energy-transfer-equity-well-known.yml.
- First-party SDKs on npm, PyPI, RubyGems, and a GitHub organization: none.
